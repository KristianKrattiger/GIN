# GIN STRAT 00 — Strategy Register

> Index and quarantine. Every go-to-market, consulting, and market-positioning claim lives here and nowhere else, so that nothing in the conceptual register (GIN 00–13) or the engineering register (GIN ENG 00–02) can be mistaken for a commercial bet — and no commercial bet can borrow the authority of a measured spec or a mechanism argument.

---

## Why this register is separate

GIN already runs a two-register discipline: the **conceptual** register argues mechanism and philosophy; the **engineering** register measures. This is a third kind of claim entirely — a *bet about the world and the market*, revised on contact with reality rather than proven by argument or measurement.

Strategy content has different epistemic status from both. A mechanism paper is right or wrong on the merits of its argument. An engineering line is unmeasured until it is measured. A strategy claim is a **wager** — about market timing, about which institution recruits first, about whether a category exists before the market names it. Wagers do not belong in the mechanism papers, where they would read as architectural claims, nor in the engineering register, where they would read as measured facts. Held apart, they can be argued, dated, and later scored against what actually happened, without contaminating the two registers that carry GIN's technical credibility.

---

## The discipline

A strategy claim is honest when it:

1. states the bet as a bet, with its assumption made explicit;
2. names the trigger or evidence that would confirm or falsify it;
3. is dated, so it can be scored against outcomes rather than quietly revised.

None of these graduate a bet into a mechanism claim or a spec. Strategy stays strategy; the boundary is one-directional.
